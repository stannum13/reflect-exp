# Reflect Lite program evidence and decision ledger

Date: 2026-08-24. Synthesis base: `97c6d00dc8ca0725e24c70a220173185329dfc16`.

## Decision

The current evidence justifies a **typed, layered synthetic reference architecture**, not completion of the Reflect Lite research program. Six bounded workstreams are accepted at their narrow scientific or engineering scopes. Exp16 supplies accepted confirmatory evidence for the preregistered direct route-boundary replication in its scripted MuJoCo matrix; Exp13 and Exp15 V2 remain separately approved authenticated portable archives whose formal dispositions are `INVALID_EXPERIMENT`. Together the program supports keeping live belief separate from durable geometry/semantics, keeping episodic events separately addressable, separating appearance-sensitive semantics from geometry, and making refresh/retrigger/replan explicit policies. It does not establish a learned VLA policy or semantic-memory hierarchy, real perception, physical transfer, generalization beyond the registered Exp16 matrix, or that recovery should always occur at the lowest sufficient level.

## What is scientifically supported now

1. **Synthetic causal:** online, separately addressable semantic and episodic memory beats a fixed snapshot in Exp04’s matched generator; geometry-derived sensing resists the appearance manipulation that defeats RGB-only detection in Exp12.
2. **Engineering:** typed twin layers, explicit provenance, and explicit trigger policies are useful implementation choices in Exp05/10/11’s frozen constructed worlds.
3. **Bounded synthetic confirmation:** Exp16 closes the direct route-boundary replication gate inside its preregistered scripted MuJoCo matrix: R3 matches R2 on success, progress, and safety-violation rate while reducing wakes. It supports no hierarchy success advantage and no causal-lowest, VLA, real-robot, or generalization claim.
4. **Directional portable only:** Exp13 and Exp15 V2 are independently approved as reconstructable archives. Both remain formal `INVALID_EXPERIMENT`; Exp15 V2 closely repeats the P6 hierarchy direction but cannot confirm it because three P4 route exceptions invalidate the frozen matrix.
5. **Rejected:** Exp14 cannot qualify a mission-memory/recovery mechanism despite replayable descriptive raw facts.
6. **Not run:** pi0.5 semantic-plan inference and the learned semantic-memory hierarchy remain open in this ledger.

## Program success-criterion disposition

The Section 36 arrow chain is **partially justified** through the memory/twin/trigger/perception seams. It is **not empirically closed** from mission language through a learned general/specialist policy to temporal execution, MPC/IK, and physical low-level control. The state stack should presently be written as:

```text
durable semantic identity + durable metric/topological geometry
        ↓ typed links, never silent overwrite
timestamped live belief/confidence/provenance
        ↔ bounded episodic events
        → layer-specific refresh / retrigger / replan / abort proposals
```

Recent robot state remains an executor input, but no accepted result in this synthesis selects its production retention policy. A learned world model has no measured bounded decision role. Unitree R1 transfer, remote GPU/VLA inference, real sensors, and physical deployment remain outside the accepted evidence.

Therefore the program status is **PARTIAL_INDICATIVE_EVIDENCE — CONTINUE**, not `COMPLETE`. Exp16 meets the fresh direct layer-recovery route-boundary replication gate: its preregistered dynamic route exhaustion became a safe-abort task failure and the valid matrix passed. The remaining immediate promotion gate is a compact checkpoint-backed learned pi0.5 study against an honestly supported semantic output/readout interface, with semantic memory present versus masked and a hard action firewall above the separate motion and control layers. Until that learned semantic-memory hierarchy is measured, the full architecture arrow diagram is not experimentally selected.
